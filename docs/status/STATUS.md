## Owner decisions needed (raised by today's work)

- **Research gate:** on highly autocorrelated candidates, the paired-bootstrap gate interval covers 92.7% instead of the 93–97% target. Choose a higher confidence level, or a minimum effective sample size, for such candidates.
- **Enterprise value:** filers usually omit preferred stock and minority interest when they have none, so EV is INCOMPLETE for most filers. Keep that, or treat an omission as none, with a flag.
- **Daily loss window:** it starts at the first valuation of each risk day, so an overnight gap counts only against the cumulative allowance. Confirm or change.
- **Stale quotes on sells:** a stale quote also refuses risk-reducing exits (QUOTE_FRESH applies to sells). Confirm against ADR-0010.
- **Unverified data:**
  - third-party sell fees are assumed at 1 bp;
  - the reuse terms of the Treasury feed are unreviewed;
  - whether the EU T+1 switch applies to trades or to settlements dated from 2027-10-11 is unconfirmed.
- **IBKR eligibility:** whether the account has EU trading permissions. NSE needs an IB India account (Indian residents or NRIs); BSE is not offered by IBKR.
- **Wave D trading-behaviour decisions, for owner review.** These were made in implementation and are recorded as ADR amendments:
  - an IBKR Inactive order is held as UNKNOWN with its reservation; a held buy is canceled automatically and a held sell is not (ADR-0009);
  - an execution redelivered under the same id with changed content is recorded but not booked until the owner resolves the case (ADR-0009);
  - a late effect never restates a completion; the owner disposes of any residue with a DISPOSAL sell (ADR-0008);
  - a stop the database cannot record is held in memory and in a file, and buys are canceled at the broker directly (ADR-0010).
- **IBKR settled cash without a SettledCash row (ADR-0012 amendment 2026-10-24).** The owner's paper account sends no `SettledCash` row, so every snapshot was incomplete and preflight could never pass. The adapter now reports settled = TotalCashValue only when `reqExecutions` (account, `lastNDays=7`) completes with zero executions and `reqAllOpenOrders` completes with no open order. The figure is recorded as `settled_source` on the snapshot and on `ReconciliationCompleted`. To confirm or change:
  - this rule itself;
  - the 7-day window, which is used because no verified holiday calendar exists, and blocks the derivation for up to a week after any trade;
  - reliance on IB Gateway honouring `lastNDays`, which is unverified (R5).
