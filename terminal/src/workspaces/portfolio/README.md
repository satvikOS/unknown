# Portfolio workspace

Read-only portfolio analytics from `/api/portfolio/*` (`backend/src/unknown/api/routes/portfolio.py` over `unknown/analytics`; CAP-PORT-01..13). Nothing on these pages reserves, orders or changes policy.

Shortcut `g f`, nav order 45. Route: `#/portfolio/<tab>`. The palette lists every tab after Holdings. The scope (account, system, or one owner such as `owner:session:<id>`) is shared across tabs. Every result shows the backend's lineage block, which lists formula ids (with their definitions from `/api/portfolio/formulas`), units, source dates, missing inputs and notes. Results computed from fixture data carry the FIXTURE label.

| Tab | Endpoint(s) | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Holdings | `holdings?scope=&basis=` (polled every 5 s: marks move without events) | NAV marked and fresh per currency, available cash, authorized capital, NAV by owner (exact share bars), the cash ladder, owners (settled, available, market value, NAV, valuation status), and positions with mark price, status, age and source. Unpriced positions are never valued at zero, and an `unpriced_positions` valuation status is shown as bad. The cash ladder and the authorized total are computed by the API over the whole account whatever the scope, and are labelled that way. |
| Performance | `performance?scope=&convention=&periods_per_year=`, `pnl?scope=` | TWR (annualized only for periods of at least a year, otherwise with the reason), Modified Dietz, the money-weighted return with a **uniqueness flag**, drawdown, the unit-value line, volatility, Sharpe with its standard error, Sortino, and sub-period returns. Daily P&L has an exact bar chart, its components and the identity check (residual exactly zero). A day with an unpriced snapshot counts in total P&L but in no component total (`analytics/pnl.py`); the panel names those days and says the components do not add up to the total. |
| Exposure and VaR | `exposure?scope=`, `var?scope=&alpha=&horizon=`, `var/backtest?scope=&alpha=` | Gross, net, long and short exposure. Weights of scope NAV, HHI, effective N and top-k share. Exposure by instrument, owner or SIC sector, and coverage. Historical VaR and ES (statistical floats, loss positive; "VaR does not represent the worst-case loss"). The backtest chart shows the forecast line, realized-loss bars and **exceptions** marked with triangles, plus Kupiec POF in words and the skipped days. |
| Stress | `stress/<overnight_gap\|correlated_selloff\|stale_quote\|fee_revision>?scope=&gap=\|shock=\|delay_s=\|factor=` | Runs only on demand. Shows the HYPOTHETICAL label, scenario P&L by currency and owner, positions revalued, loss thresholds, the core's reaction on its private copy, and the scenario-specific findings: orders refused on stale quotes, the fresh NAV before and after and the haircut values; for a fee revision, the pending estimates finalised at the revised fee with their additional cost, open buy orders whose fee envelope would be under-reserved, exit fees, and the core's reaction (broker commissions booked on the private copy, available cash and open exceptions afterwards, never a stop-latch claim). Positions a shock could not revalue (unpriced) are listed next to the scenario P&L, which leaves them out. Each threshold says what its level is compared with: a session's equity after the scenario (crossed at or below), or the account loss from the risk day's start or the P&L high-water mark (crossed at or above), shown in its own column. A threshold the backend could not decide (an unpriced position) shows **unknown** with the reason, never "no". The daily window's start and the backend's alternative window are listed under the table. |
| What-if | `whatif?session=&instrument=&side=&quantity=&limit=` | Runs only on demand. Shows the HYPOTHETICAL label, would-be approved or refused, every pretrade check (`orders/DecisionTable`), the quote evaluated, a reservation preview (nothing is held), cash and quantity impact, and figures if completely filled. |
| Allocation | `allocation?scope=&cash_reserve=&floor=&cap=&eta=&tau=` | The PROPOSAL ONLY label. Current weights and the targets of equal weight, inverse volatility and mean-variance, plus covariance diagnostics. For a session scope, a whole-lot trade list per method with every constraint check, flagged when its sizing prices are stale marks. The page offers no way to send a trade. |
| Snapshots | `snapshots?limit=` | The valuation runs the time series reads, as visible now (revisions and the FIXTURE flag). |

## Rules this workspace keeps

- **Money stays exact.** Amounts are the API's decimal strings, shown with `Money`. Bar lengths and shares use BigInt ratios (`exactBars`, `exactShares`, `weightPct`). Returns, volatilities, VaR/ES, optimiser weights and test statistics are floats that the backend computed from exact inputs. They are labelled as statistics and are never used as money.
- **Every amount has its currency.** What-if headroom is formatted in the session currency. Threshold rows carry no currency in the response. Session rows take the session's currency from `pnl_by_owner` (the backend builds both from the same owner and currency pairs); account rows take the account's base currency from `/api/overview`. When that is ambiguous, the exact amount is shown as "currency not reported".
- **IRR uniqueness.** A money-weighted rate is shown only when exactly one root solves the cash-flow equation. "Not unique" (several sign changes) and "no solution" are flagged in red with the backend's reason. Periods under one year show only the period MWR (GIPS 2.A.12).
- **Hypothetical results are not recomputed silently.** What-if and stress results are fetched once per run, not on every stream version bump. They show the core sequence they were computed on, and a "Stale" notice with a Re-run button once the core records a later event (`whatif.is_stale`).
- **No snapshot history yet.** The tab shows the backend's own reason, including the `snapshots record` command, and no placeholder figures.
- **Charts** are inline SVG with no dependency. Each one is a summary of a table on the same page. Sign and exceptions are shown by position and shape as well as colour.

## API gaps found

- The money-weighted result has no explicit uniqueness field. The terminal reads `irr_annualized`, `period_mwr` and the `reason` text (`irrStatus`). An explicit `roots` count would be sturdier.
- Stress threshold rows carry no currency (see above).
- Fee-revision `exit_costs` and stale-quote `conservative_values` carry no currency; the terminal takes the position's currency from the instrument master. `open_buy_envelopes` carry neither a currency nor an instrument, so their amounts are shown as "currency not reported".
- There is no formula-to-capability link in the API, other than the `capabilities` field of `/api/portfolio/formulas` entries.
- The icon is the generic one; `ui/Icon.tsx` has no portfolio glyph and is outside this workspace's scope.
